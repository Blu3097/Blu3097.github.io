01 · Research

Physics in AdS, from fields to strings.

A recurring theme in my work is to use anti-de Sitter space as a controlled setting in which analytic structure, symmetry, and dynamics can be studied together — and then related back to flat-space scattering.

01

Gauge dynamics in AdS

I am currently studying perturbative gauge theory on fixed AdS backgrounds, with boundary CFT observables as probes of weak- and strong-coupling dynamics. This includes boundary correlators, AdS unitarity, and the emergence of flat-space scattering in the large-radius limit.

02

String amplitudes & exact structures

I study string amplitudes in AdS and the structures that organize them: high-energy and Regge limits, KLT and monodromy relations, factorization, and worldsheet-inspired descriptions of holographic observables.

03

Conformal field theory

My work also uses analytic and numerical bootstrap methods, lightcone expansions, chiral algebra, and defect/boundary CFT techniques to extract and organize dynamical information in conformal theories.
